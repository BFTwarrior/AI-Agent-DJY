- 2026-11-01 auto-continue: a retryable turn failure (ECONNRESET/timeout/rate limit/provider 5xx) no longer creates a "（重试）" task. Daemon, captain, reviewer license and `purpose=red_auto_review` contractors may continue; plain members get `403`. Review rows are hidden from the team payload, task list, panel snapshot and audit, always finish `completed` with an `outcome`, and are summarized as `redReviews`.
